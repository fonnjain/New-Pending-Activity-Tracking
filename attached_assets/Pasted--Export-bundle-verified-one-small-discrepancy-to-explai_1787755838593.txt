## The one thing to explain

**Activity Wise shows FG as 17,714 marks / 4,707.752 MT.** The source file's TLT FG is **17,692
marks / 4,701.623 MT** — a difference of **+22 marks / +6.129 MT**.

It is not a straight TLT/NTLT mix: NTLT FG is 165 marks / 4,146.774 MT, so it is neither included
whole nor excluded whole.

```sql
-- what are the 22?
SELECT rp.job, rp.order_nature, rp.type, rp.activity,
       count(*) AS marks, sum(rp.balance_wt)/1000 AS mt
  FROM record_pool rp
  JOIN import_rows ir ON ...
 WHERE ir.import_id = <the 26-Aug WIP import>
   AND <whatever predicate the Activity Wise FG row uses>
 GROUP BY 1,2,3,4;
```

Please report what the Activity Wise FG row actually selects on, and which 22 marks it picks up
that a plain `Order Nature = Structure AND Type = FG Pending For Dispatch` filter does not.

Most likely candidates: NTLT FG rows leaking in on a partial predicate, or marks with a blank
Type being swept into FG. Either is small, but a report whose FG differs from the WIP file's FG by
an unexplained amount will be queried by someone eventually.

## Two conventions worth documenting rather than changing

Both are correct. Both would confuse a reader comparing bundles.

**Weights are in kilograms in this bundle, MT in the Project Wise export.** Activity Wise shows
RFI as 1,426,262.983 — that is 1,426.263 MT and matches source exactly. Consistent with the raw
file, inconsistent with the other export. Worth a line in the README stating the unit, rather than
changing either.

**Activity Wise covers 55,680 marks, not 70,642.** The difference is exactly 14,962 — Release
Balance 10,733 plus Awaiting Assignment 4,229. It shows only marks that have reached an activity,
and its `C` row is the Cutting bucket alone at 1,267.635 MT. Correct, but someone will reconcile
it against the six-bucket total and find a 14,962-mark gap. One line in the README would prevent
that.

## WHAT NOT TO CHANGE

- Do NOT change the kg/MT conventions in either export. Document them.
- Do NOT change what Activity Wise scopes to. Document it.
- Do NOT change bucket definitions, `PROCESS_SEQUENCE`, the `P`/`S` alias, `hashRow`,
  `classifyDc17`, the NTLT model, the eight material columns, the normaliser, or the regression
  guard.
- Do NOT alter the FG predicate before reporting what it currently selects.
