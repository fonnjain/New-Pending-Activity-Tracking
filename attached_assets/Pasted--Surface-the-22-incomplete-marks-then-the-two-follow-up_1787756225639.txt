## PART 1 — the 22 marks are incomplete records, not finished goods

Every one of them is:

```
Order Nature      Structure
Type              Job Card WIP
Job Card Status   Authorized
Activity          blank
Job Card No.      blank
Contractor        blank
```

A mark in production with **no job card number and no contractor** is not finished goods. Activity
Wise reports them as FG only because blank activity is displayed as FG — correct for genuine FG
rows, misleading for these.

Two patterns in the list suggest they are a real category rather than random bad rows:

**Every mark number ends in `W` or `AW`** — 3W, 5W, 6W, 1W, 2W, 1AW, 2AW. That is a naming
convention, not a coincidence.

**Thirteen of the twenty-two are in project 920**, across six structures — 3BPI1, 3BPI3, 3CT1,
3CT2, 3HISO, 3HLA, 3LA, 4ISO1. The rest are spread one or two each across 824, 848, 906 and VS-33.

`W` and `AW` most likely mean welded or welded-assembly. The WIP file already carries an
`Is Welded Structure` column, which makes that testable.

### What to check

```sql
SELECT rp.is_welded_structure, count(*) AS marks,
       sum(rp.balance_wt)/1000 AS mt
  FROM record_pool rp
  JOIN import_rows ir ON ...
 WHERE ir.import_id = 86
   AND rp.job = 'Structure'
   AND rp.type = 'Job Card WIP'
   AND (rp.activity IS NULL OR rp.activity = '')
 GROUP BY 1;
```

If all 22 carry `Is Welded Structure = True`, that is the explanation and it can be documented
rather than investigated. If they are a mix, it is a data-entry gap.

Also report how many marks in the whole import have `Is Welded Structure = True` — the 26-Aug file
has a few hundred — and how many of those have a blank job card. That distinguishes "welded items
are handled outside the job-card flow" from "these 22 are simply incomplete".

### What to change

**Nothing in the six-bucket logic.** It already handles them correctly — the blank-activity
catch-all puts them in Quality Check, which is where a `Job Card WIP` row belongs. No reported
figure is wrong.

**In Activity Wise**, separate them from genuine FG. Either a distinct row labelled *Blank activity
(no job card)* with its own count and weight, or the existing FG row with a footnote naming the
count. Do not merge them silently into FG — a report that shows 6.129 MT of unstarted work as
finished is the kind of thing that is believed until someone goes looking for the material.

**Add a Data Check warning** — not a hard rule — reporting `Job Card WIP` rows with a blank job
card number. Today it is 22 marks and 6.129 MT. If it grows, somebody should see it.
