## What to check in the reply

- **That the Total fix names the export path specifically.** Reporting "already fixed"
  means it looked at the on-screen code again; the export is a separate path and is
  demonstrably still broken.
- **That 956 and 963 appear** after the threshold fix, with a No-BOM-match cause footnote.
- **A real number for Defect 3(b).** The batch swap on 900 and 933 accounts for roughly
  4 MT net, and the two missing projects for 181 MT. That leaves about 170 MT unexplained
  across 28 projects, which is the part that matters.
- **No code changes on Defect 3 before the diagnosis.** Adjusting figures to reconcile
  without knowing why would hide the cause.
