On the Fabrication Report – Project Completion (TLT), please add a Total column as the
last column, covering Cols D through M.

Important: the total must NOT be a plain sum of D through M, because column E
(Assignment Balance) overlaps other columns and would be double counted.

Assignment Balance is not a production stage. It is "Job Card Not Started AND Contractor
is blank", which is a cross-cutting lens on marks already counted elsewhere:
  - its Initial portion is already in D (Release Balance)
  - its Authorized portion is already in F (Cutting)

Verified on the 28-Jul file: 100% of Release Balance marks have a blank contractor, so all
of D sits inside E, and E splits exactly into 2,277.436 MT Initial (all of D) plus
1,033.439 MT Authorized (inside F). E adds no new weight at all.

So the formula is:

    Total = D + F + G + H + I + J + K + L + M      (skip E)

All the other columns are mutually exclusive: D and F split Not Started by Job Card Status,
G through M are Job Card WIP with one activity per mark, and Not Started never overlaps
Job Card WIP. E is the only overlapping column.

Please also:

1. Label the column so the exclusion is visible, e.g. "Total Fabrication Balance
   (excl. Assignment)", with a tooltip or footnote explaining that Assignment Balance is
   omitted because it double counts Release Balance and Cutting.

2. Make sure it is correct whether Special Ops is collapsed or expanded — J, K and L must
   be counted once in total, not once collapsed and again expanded.

3. Include the new column in the Excel export, and add it to the Sub-Type subtotal, BOM
   Label total and Grand Total rows using the same formula, not by summing the D:M totals.

On the 28-Jul data the Grand Total of the new column should be about 6,416 MT. A plain
D:M sum would give about 9,724 MT, which is roughly 34% too high — that gap is the check
that the exclusion is working.

Do not change any existing column or any underlying calculation. This is an added display
column only.

When done, paste the total-column expression and the subtotal/grand-total handling
verbatim.
