## What to watch for in the reply

- **A count for `active = false` on this import.** If it is not 353 / 330.258 MT, the
  hypothesis is dead and the answer must come from elsewhere.
- **A direct answer on whether Job Wise calls `filterRecords`.** The two facts as stated —
  "every page uses filterRecords" and "Job Wise shows all 58,844 rows" — cannot both be
  true.
- **Substantiation for "superseded".** If a pool row was superseded, its replacement should
  be counted, and the total would not drop.
- **No code changes.** This is a diagnosis request; a fix applied before the cause is known
  risks hiding the discrepancy rather than resolving it.
