REPLIT AGENT PROMPT — MOVE TS INTO FABRICATION BUNDLES; GALVANIZING = G, GB ONLY
================================================================================
Update the activity bundle definitions: TS (Tee Stock) moves OUT of the Galvanizing bundle
and INTO the two TLT fabrication bundles as their last step. Galvanizing becomes G and GB
only. Yard unchanged. Display/filter change to bundle membership only; do not change parsing,
sequences, ageing, dedup, classification, the warning math, or the fabrication-load report
rules (those reference specific activities, not the Galvanizing bundle).

TLT SEQUENCE (reference, unchanged): C, RFI, NH, B, HAB, HG, W, Q, TS, G, GB, Y.

NEW BUNDLE DEFINITIONS
  id "TLT_FABRICATION"          label "Fabrication (TLT)"
      activities = [C, RFI, NH, B, HAB, HG, W, Q, TS]      (C -> TS; now INCLUDES TS)
  id "TLT_FAB_PENDING_QUALITY"  label "Fab - Pending Quality (TLT)"
      activities = [RFI, NH, B, HAB, HG, W, Q, TS]         (RFI -> TS; now INCLUDES TS)
  id "GALVANIZING"  label "Galvanizing"   activities = [G, GB]    (TS REMOVED; G, GB only)
  id "YARD"         label "Yard"          activities = [Y]        (unchanged)

So the contiguous boundary between fabrication and galvanizing moves one step later:
fabrication now ends at TS; galvanizing starts at G.

SCOPE / NTLT HANDLING
  - Fabrication bundles remain TLT only (namespaced with "TLT").
  - GALVANIZING (now G, GB) and YARD remain ALL order types (shared).
  - NTLT: since the fabrication bundles are TLT-only and TS is no longer in Galvanizing,
    TS becomes a STANDALONE activity for NTLT — it belongs to NO bundle in NTLT mode.
    In NTLT mode the activity dropdown shows: Galvanizing (G, GB) and Yard (Y) bundles, then
    the NTLT individual activities (NTF, NTFSW, NTFW, TS, G, GB, Y) in sequence order, with
    TS selectable on its own (no bundle).
  - In TLT mode the dropdown shows: Fabrication (TLT) [C..TS], Fab - Pending Quality (TLT)
    [RFI..TS], Galvanizing [G, GB], Yard [Y], then individual TLT activities.

UPDATE EVERYWHERE THE BUNDLES ARE USED
  - The single bundles constants module (keyed by id) — change the activity sets as above.
    Everything importing it updates automatically.
  - Plant Operation Wise -> Fabrication tab (TLT): its scope is the Fabrication bundle, so it
    now INCLUDES TS marks. The Galvanization tab scope is now G, GB only (TS no longer here).
    Confirm the tab scopes follow the new bundle sets.
  - Any activity-bundle filter/dropdown reflects the new membership.

DO NOT CHANGE (guard against over-reach)
  - The TLT sequence order itself (TS still sits between Q and G in the sequence).
  - The "Fabrication Load for TLT" report/dashboard column rules — they key off SPECIFIC
    activities (W, B, RFI, C) and section/hole rules, NOT the Galvanizing bundle, so they are
    unaffected. Leave them as-is.
  - Ageing, warning thresholds, velocity, milestones, thickness, classification.
