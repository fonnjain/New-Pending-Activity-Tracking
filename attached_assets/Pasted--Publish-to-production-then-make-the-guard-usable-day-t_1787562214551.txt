## PART 2 — make the guard usable day to day

Only after Part 1 is reported. **Nothing about what blocks changes.**

### Why

I advised overriding the 22-August Order Review and wrote a confident reason calling it a
source-data correction. **That was wrong.** VTPL later explained it: a packing list dated
29-06-2026 was deleted and re-authorised by the Dispatch Department, and the export was taken
mid-transaction. Had we overridden, production would hold 291.882 MT permanently with an audit
note explaining an event that never happened.

What saved us is that **cumulative files are self-healing**. Skipping 22-August cost nothing —
23-August carried the corrected 326.511 MT. Overriding is irreversible in a way skipping is not.

### 2.1 Recommend the reversible action

Add a **recommended action** to the staging panel, chosen by the signature classifier:

| Situation | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Cause not known at upload time | **Skip this file.** Tomorrow's export supersedes it. |
| Signature A — cancellation or transfer | Override **only if** the cancellation is confirmed. Otherwise skip. |
| Signature B — restatement or correction | **Skip unless the cause is known.** Verify against tomorrow's file. |
| Signature C — unrecognised, or many projects | **Do not import.** Wrong file, not an event. |

Advice, not obstruction. The override path stays exactly as it is. Add one line wherever it is
offered:

> Skipping is reversible — the next day's file carries the corrected cumulative figures.
> Overriding is not.
